With eggs, tomatoes, and green onions, you can make 2 fantastic comfort dishes in just 15 minutes:

1. Floating Cloud Egg & Tomato Soup (Canh Trứng Cà Chua)

Sauté chopped tomatoes until tender and vibrant red, add boiling broth, gently swirl in 2 whisked eggs to form delicate cloud ribbons. Garnish with chopped scallions and fragrant ground pepper.

2. Homestyle Stir-Fried Eggs with Tomatoes

Soft-scramble eggs gently until fluffy, then set aside. Simmer ripe tomatoes with a dash of fish sauce and a pinch of sugar into a glossy sauce, then toss back the scrambled eggs for 30 seconds.
